# Voice streaming validation

## Status and collection

The host implementation includes incremental model delivery, one bounded continuous PCM output per answer, cross-fragment synthesis prefetch and playback-based completion. After explicit user authorization on 2026-10-16, the local pipeline was deployed and the device service restarted; configuration, dependencies and the local default backend were preserved. Real HAL playback/drain/cancellation and microphone smoke checks passed. See the [deployment record](voice-streaming-deployment-2026-10-16.md). Board acoustic acceptance and comparative p50/p95 sampling remain pending; callback consumption does not establish measured speaker sound.

Enable `LIRA_VOICE_METRICS=1` before starting the process. The default is disabled. In a diagnostic harness, read `runtime.voice_metrics.snapshot()` for raw numeric stage records or `.summary()` for per-utterance timings. Export outside playback callbacks. Rings retain at most 2048 records, dropping the oldest; `dropped_events` counts records skipped to avoid lock contention. Export frequently enough to retain complete samples; incomplete stages yield null, never zero. Do not treat missing measurements as success.

Only numeric fields and structural utterance/turn/owner identifiers are accepted. No text, PCM, credentials or persistent second audio log is added. Privacy activation clears the ring, advances its epoch, and rejects events from outstanding old traces even after privacy is disabled. Events are captured with their originating trace instead of a global current owner.

## Measurement contract

- `asr_submit`: dispatch acceptance timestamp, not the human speech endpoint.
- `llm_request` / `llm_content`: model request and first nonblank visible text. Empty and role-only deltas do not count. Repeated model requests remain visible in raw records.
- `segment_submit`, `synthesis_start`, `synthesis_end`, `pcm_ready`: enqueue and real worker execution. Synthesis timing includes the complete generation call, including prefetched calls; awaiting an already running prefetch is separately named `chunk_wait`, never interpreted as generation latency. RTF is compute seconds divided by generated audio seconds.
- `output_consumed`: first HAL callback consuming nonempty PCM. This estimates audio device submission, not measured sound at the speaker. Wake chime and progress/ack speech have answer=false; streamed answer and final answer use answer=true.
- `output_drained` / `cancel_requested` / `cancel_confirmed`: output lifecycle and cancellation. Confirmation of a cancelled synthesis-only request means no audio output remains, not that native compute was preempted.
- `buffer` supports buffer frames, device underflow, producer starvation duration/count as separate measurements. The continuous driver counts a starvation episode when requested frames exceed prepared PCM, and accumulates its duration separately from device underflow. Audio-content silence is not producer starvation. Final callback padding after finite EOF is not starvation.

At each phase record device and OS, selected model/backend, model revision, sample rate, TTS threads, network configuration excluding credentials, warm/cold status, fixed sample identifiers, failure counts, CPU/RSS and all raw numeric samples. Current repository defaults are not deployment evidence.

Use the same device/network and fixed corpus: at least 30 ordinary questions, 10 search/clarification questions, and five continuous reading samples of at least two minutes per configuration. Report p50/p95 with sample sizes and failures, startup separately, device underflows separately from producer starvation count/duration, longest nonsemantic pause, cancellation-to-silence, and acoustic loopback or external recording calibration. Human speech-end-to-first-sound requires an external timestamp. Software timestamp tests do not replace acoustic testing.

Integration gates so far: U1 focused gate **176 passed**; U2/U3 text, imports and HAL gate **108 passed**; U4 audio gate **38 passed, 1 deselected** (known KWS case), followed by the number/date duration case **1 passed**; U5 dialog/state/reading/privacy gate **244 passed**. Mock PCM consumption is not measured sound.

U6 worker evidence: its initial six proof tests failed against the separate planning path; its focused gate then passed 81 tests. A non-search voice question now needs one model request instead of two. A searched answer retains tool selection plus a final model answer (two model requests, with the search service between them). This removes the extra planning request but does not prove early sound for the uncertain initial stream. Authoritative integrated U6 text/model gate: **93 passed**. Authoritative U7 provider/configuration/privacy/main/session/HAL/dialog gate: **142 passed**. ## Trial and rollback checklist (pending execution)

Owner: the authorized board-trial operator, not yet assigned. The observation window is the full fixed corpus plus both ten-minute echo/noise runs. Compare `llm event=reasoning_first`, `candidate_content`, `tool_stream_confirmed`, `search_done`, and `tts event=synthesis_ready` with exported numeric records; log timing alone does not measure sound. Expect non-tool streaming output before generation ends, one output stream per successful answer, bounded queues and no completed history after failed playback. Buffering an uncertain tool-selection stream is expected.

Stop the trial and restore the verified artifacts if self-playback triggers an appliance action, cancelled-owner audio returns, privacy permits a new remote send, reading skips a block, or stop/output failure leaves playback uncontrolled. Record the trigger and affected sample. If local synthesis RTF remains at or above one, record the resulting starvation and keep that backend outside continuity acceptance; buffering cannot fix sustained insufficient synthesis throughput. Use the plan's numerical targets for the fixed-corpus performance decision rather than inventing thresholds from host mocks.
